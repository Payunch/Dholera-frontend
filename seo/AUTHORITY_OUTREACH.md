# Authority and backlink outreach package

## Link-worthy assets to produce

- A source-linked Dholera infrastructure milestone timeline with revision history.
- A plain-language guide to reading TP/DP maps, including limitations and official verification paths.
- A maintained table of major announced projects with announcement date, source, status date, and correction log.
- Original field photographs and observations with capture dates and locations.

## Suitable outreach targets

- Gujarat business and infrastructure journalists.
- Urban planning, logistics, semiconductor, renewable-energy, and industrial trade publications.
- Property-law, title-due-diligence, architecture, and civil-engineering contributors.
- Relevant chambers, professional associations, universities, and research groups.

## Rules

- Personalize outreach and lead with evidence useful to the recipient.
- Request editorial consideration, never a guaranteed followed link.
- Disclose commercial relationships.
- Do not buy bulk links, exchange sitewide links, automate spam, or use irrelevant directories.
- Record contact, asset, date, response, resulting mention, and referral traffic.
