# Dholera Platform SEO Improvement Plan

**Started:** 01-09-2026  
**Primary website:** https://www.dholeraplatform.com  
**Primary objective:** Grow qualified organic traffic for Dholera planning, infrastructure, investment-research, and project-status searches. Ranking first for the single word `Dholera` is a long-term ambition, not the initial success metric.

## Safety and quality rules

- Keep one canonical host everywhere: `https://www.dholeraplatform.com`.
- Preserve existing user work and avoid unrelated changes.
- Do not publish invented facts, dates, returns, endorsements, or unverifiable claims.
- Treat investment content as high-trust content: cite primary sources, identify the author/reviewer, and state risks clearly.
- Verify changes with lint/build and live URL checks before marking them complete.
- Deploy before submitting updated URLs or the sitemap in Google Search Console.
