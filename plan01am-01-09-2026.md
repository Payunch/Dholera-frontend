# Dholera Platform Search Growth Plan

**Prepared:** 01:00 AM, 01-09-2026  
**Baseline source:** `https___dholeraplatform.com_-Performance-on-Search-2026-09-01.xlsx`  
**Reporting filter:** Web Search, last 6 months  
**Preferred website:** https://www.dholeraplatform.com

## Objective

Expand Google visibility beyond the homepage by building useful, source-linked pages for the search intents already appearing in Search Console. The immediate goal is not rank #1 for the single word `Dholera`; it is to earn consistent impressions and qualified clicks for maps, city facts, connectivity, infrastructure, planning, and due-diligence searches.

## Guardrails

- Do not buy bulk backlinks or create doorway/misspelling pages.
- Do not publish unverifiable completion dates, prices, returns, or endorsements.
- Do not confuse the 109.019 km expressway corridor length with every Ahmedabad-to-Dholera journey distance.
- Do not offer copyrighted or restricted documents for download without permission.
- Do not change several pages to target the same query.
- Do not evaluate success from one-impression queries or day-to-day ranking movement.
